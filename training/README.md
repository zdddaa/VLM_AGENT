# Training

Offline-only training area.

Planned stages:

1. construct object-centric T1/T2 image-text instructions;
2. quality-filter semantic and verification instructions;
3. fine-tune the temporal multimodal VLM with SFT/LoRA;
4. evaluate T1 semantics, T2 semantics, transition consistency, real-vs-pseudo change, and calibration;
5. keep training labels and human audit information out of online inference state.
